# peteoshea.co.uk

Pete O'Shea's personal website: a CV, an about page and written posts grouped by subject.

## Language

**Section**:
One of the subject areas of written content (Ramblings, Systems, Personal), shown as a page with a header image, a description and its list of Posts. A Section with no Posts is not shown.
_Avoid_: Blog, category, topic

**Post**:
A single piece of writing belonging to exactly one Section. It always has a title, and may have a date, a summary and a Post image; whatever it lacks is simply not shown.
_Avoid_: Blog post, article, entry

**Post image**:
The optional main picture for a Post, which represents it as a whole. Pictures that only illustrate a point inside the writing are not Post images.
_Avoid_: Hero image, featured image, thumbnail

**Old Blog**:
Pete's previous website, whose posts are being brought across as Posts.
_Avoid_: Legacy site, archive
